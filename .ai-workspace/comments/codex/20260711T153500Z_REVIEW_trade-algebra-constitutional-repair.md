# Review: Trade Algebra Constitutional Repair

Date: 2026-07-12
Change class: `intent_reprice`
Verdict: accept with repairs applied

## Accepted Findings

1. Treatment application was formalized while treatment discovery, authorship,
   entity resolution, evidence, review, and maintenance cost were assumed.
2. One temporal-role vocabulary flattened domain economic time together with
   audit and knowledge time; correction was incorrectly adjacent to both.
3. The commodity fixture proved semantic plumbing but could not qualify broad
   CDM lifecycle-spine claims.
4. The pivot from the bootstrap forensic-attribution engine to the contextual
   trade algebra lacked explicit constitutional lineage.

## Repairs

- Treatment evidence, candidate generation, authorship, admission, bounded
  scope, reuse, repair, cost, and supersession are first-class product surfaces.
- Domain temporal coordinates, audit/knowledge temporal coordinates, and
  correction relations are orthogonal. Correction is evidence-backed
  supersession, not a temporal-role enum value.
- The seven-date commodity fixture is explicitly semantic-plumbing proof. A
  separate CDM lifecycle qualification slice covers partial fills, allocation,
  amendment, novation, multi-trade netting or compression, and a relevant
  corporate-action event.
- `INTENT.md` and `PRODUCT.md` explicitly supersede the bootstrap's provisional
  intent and product while retaining forensic attribution as one projection.
- `GOALS.md` blocks requirement derivation until temporal semantics and the
  treatment lifecycle are ratified.

## Boundary

No requirement family or tenant implementation was created. The TypeScript
tenant remains candidate-only. G-001 through G-003 are the activation gate.
